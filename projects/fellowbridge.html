The Platform

FellowBridge helps students and researchers discover global opportunities and learn from people who have successfully navigated them, connecting them with funded opportunities and with mentors who have already been there.

I worked on two sides of the platform: the data pipeline that gathers scholarship opportunities from across the web, and the back-end that serves the site.

Data Ingestion

Scholarship opportunities are spread across many sites in many formats. I built the ingestion pipeline that scrapes these sources and lands the results in Amazon S3 as a data lake, with Amazon Athena on top so the opportunity data can be queried with SQL.

Back-end

I also worked on the platform's back-end, writing TypeScript services on AWS Lambda that serve the endpoints behind the site.
